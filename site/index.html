The pigs leave alive

1990 – 2025 · million head per year

The pig herd has been roughly flat for two decades, but what happens to it has inverted. Danish slaughterhouses killed 22.2 million pigs in 2004 and 14.8 million in 2025, while exports of live pigs — overwhelmingly weaners of about 30 kg, sent to be fattened in Germany and Poland — went from 8,800 head in 1990 to 16.7 million. In 2023, for the first time, more pigs left Denmark alive than were slaughtered in it.

Fewer cows, more milk

1990 – 2025 · index, 1990 = 100

The clearest case of animals being replaced by productivity. Denmark milked 753,000 cows in 1990 and 549,000 in 2025 — a 27% fall — yet delivered 23% more milk, because the average cow went from 6,300 kg a year to 10,600 kg. Cow numbers had already been falling for half a century before that, from a peak of 1.8 million in 1933.

The herd stayed, the farms went

One line that ends

2010 – 2021 · breeding animals

Fur animals — almost entirely mink — were counted alongside the rest until 2020. The July 2020 census found 2.23 million breeding animals on 817 farms; that November the entire national herd was ordered culled after a coronavirus variant was found in mink, and the series stops. Denmark had been the world's largest mink-pelt producer.

How to read this

method & caveats

A headcount, not a harvest

Every figure is the stock of live animals standing on farms on one census day, not annual production. Broilers live about six weeks, so the 13.8 million standing in 2024 turn over many times: 106 million chickens were slaughtered in 2025.

Two registers, joined

1920–1981 comes from the historic census (published in thousands, so those years are rounded to the nearest thousand); 1982 onward from the farm register. The join is visible as a small step in some series.

Sheep, goats and horses

Before 1982 sheep and goats were counted together. Horses from 1982 include only those on agricultural holdings — the riding and leisure horses that make up most of the modern population are not in this series.

Missing years

Poultry and sheep were not counted in a handful of interwar years, and poultry for 2025 was not yet published when this was compiled. Lines connect across those gaps; the table leaves them blank.

Data retrieved from the Statistics Denmark StatBank API: HDYR1920 (livestock 1920–1981), HDYR1 (farms with livestock, 1982–2025), PELS11 (fur animals), ANI5 (pig slaughterings and live exports), ANI7 (milk), HISB3 (population).
