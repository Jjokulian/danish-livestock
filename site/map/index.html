About the numbers

A CHR site is a place where animals are kept, not a company — one business can hold several, and 10,147 sites keep more than one species, so they appear once per species. Herd size is the June count in the register. DE (dyreenheder) is the manure-regulation animal unit and is left at zero for species outside that regulation, horses included.

Fish farms are in the register too, and they carry 45 million of the animals counted under “all registered sites” — pick a species to keep the totals comparable.

Two land figures, because they measure different things. Hectares farmed is every parcel the site's CVR number declared for area support in 2025 — it includes rented ground. Hectares held as property is the cadastral extent of the samlet faste ejendom the farmyard stands on, from the matrikel. Neither names an owner: that register needs a login.
